# Positioning one-pager

## Category

Validator intelligence and staking optimization for the BitTensor ecosystem.

## Problem

TAO holders lack reliable validator comparison, historical reward context, and risk-aware allocation insight. Existing surfaces skew toward wallet visibility or raw chain exploration.

## Solution

StakeMind is a trusted analytics layer that helps users understand validator risk, track rewards, compare performance, and prepare staking decisions safely.

## What we are building first

- Read-only wallet-connected dashboard.
- Validator discovery and comparison.
- Reward and stake visibility with historical snapshots.
- Documented ingestion from subtensor RPC and index-friendly storage.

## What we are not building first

- Full wallet replacement.
- Autonomous staking or reallocation.
- Multi-chain aggregation or institutional custody.

## Differentiation

- Validator intelligence over basic staking buttons.
- Transparent scoring inputs and repeatable ingestion.
- Non-custodial architecture with audit-friendly event logging.

## Success for Phase 1

Build scope, integrations, and data shape are validated before dashboard implementation begins in Phase 2.
