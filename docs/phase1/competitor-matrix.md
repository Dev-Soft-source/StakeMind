## Feature comparison notes

- **Wallet-first tools** optimize signing and visibility, not cross-validator risk scoring or allocation insight.
- **Explorers** expose chain data but rarely package staking decisions with consistent scoring and portfolio context.
- **Developer APIs** are integration primitives; StakeMind must add aggregation, caching, and user-facing intelligence.
- **Automation** is intentionally deferred until analytics quality and non-custodial trust are proven.

## Implications

- Lead with validator intelligence, reward history, and risk framing.
- Avoid competing as a full wallet replacement in early phases.
- Invest in documented scoring inputs, repeatable ingestion, and transparent limitations.
